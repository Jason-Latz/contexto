You did not learn your first language from flashcards. You learned it out in the world, meeting each new word wrapped in a sentence you already understood, and guessing the rest. That quiet inference, repeated a thousand times, is how fluency is built. Contexto brings the same immersion to the pages you already read, a few words at a time, in your target language, in context.

This is the actual extension behavior: a dial from your reading to gentle immersion. And yes, the first word to turn is where the name came from.

The idea

How people actually learn a target language

Drills teach you about the language.

Classes, flashcards, and quizzes build knowledge of rules. Fluency grows differently: you meet new words in sentences you mostly understand, then use the surrounding words to figure them out.

You already do this.

You learned your first language this way, and you still guess unfamiliar words from their surroundings every day. Contexto simply points that same instinct at your target language.

Immersion, without moving abroad.

You can't always live in Madrid (womp womp), but you already read for hours a day. Contexto turns that ordinary reading into gentle, dosed immersion, a few words at a time.

How it works

Four steps, then it fades into your reading

  1. 1

    Add Contexto to Chrome and pick your target language.

  2. 2

    Read any page as usual.

  3. 3

    A few words change into your target language, in context, like casa.

  4. 4

    Hover to see the original; click to save it, like palabra, and review later.

Everything runs fully offline, on your device. Nothing you read leaves your browser.

Questions

Frequently asked

Is it free?

yuh

Does my reading leave my device?

No. Contexto runs entirely offline; it never sends page text, browsing history, or translations to any server. See the privacy page.

Can I choose my target language?

Yep. Pick your target language in the extension and switch any time. If yours is not there yet, DM me on LinkedIn :)

How is this different from flashcards?

Flashcards quiz you out of context; Contexto teaches in context, the way immersion does, with no quizzes required (an optional review exists).
